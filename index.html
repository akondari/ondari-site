How it works

Set up once.
Then stay out of your own way.

Your proxy handles the rest — answering from your knowledge, learning from every escalation, and interrupting you less over time.

01

Open your channel

Install Ondari in Slack and name it (#ask-charlie, #hey-parker) — whoever your team already reaches for.

02

Feed it what you know

Drop in your SOPs, connect Google Docs, pull Slack history. The more context, the smarter the proxy.

03

It answers. You don't.

Every reply lands with a confidence score and the source it came from.

04

It learns the edges

Every escalation you resolve becomes an answer next time. The handle rate climbs; your distractions fall; your focus time is protected.

doesn't know.

You set the threshold. It respects it.

Every response carries a confidence score from 0–100%. Above your line, Ondari answers and cites its source. Below it, it tags you once — in the thread, with the reason it couldn't answer — and never guesses in your name.

Pin certain topics to always route to you, regardless of confidence: budget calls, personnel, anything that should never be automatic.
